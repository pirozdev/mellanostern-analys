> "Partial access (e.g. tankers but not strike aircraft) = AMBIGUOUS-RESOLVE-AS-NO."

The situation is more accurately: access RESTORED IN PRINCIPLE but OPERATIONAL USE NOT CONFIRMED. This falls squarely within the ambiguity rule's intent.
