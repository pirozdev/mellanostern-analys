## DA opposing narrative — Judge ruling (weekly adjudication, Monday May 18)

### Block 1 — N4 potential OBE-resolution
**PARTIAL ACCEPT / REJECT OBE-RESOLUTION**

DA claimed N4 should resolve YES based on Wikipedia OSINT of a fourth Oman round circa May 11 with both governments acknowledging, plus a fifth Rome round scheduled May 23.

**Judge ruling:** The OBE-RESOLVE-YES finding is REJECTED. Resolution criteria for N4 requires "Reuters, AP, or AFP newswire confirmation; OR Axios with named-official attribution from both governments." Wikipedia is an OSINT source that aggregates secondary and tertiary content; it does not independently meet the tier-1 confirmation threshold even when citing Reuters. The operative test is whether a direct Reuters/AP wire story with named US and Iranian officials confirming the meeting exists in the primary sources — it does not appear in Källa A's 15 TIER1 sources, and Analyst found no such confirmation.

**However:** The OSINT evidence is substantively credible and represents a meaningful counter-signal to Analyst's full −17pp reduction. A backchannel that produced a fourth Oman round and has a fifth Rome meeting scheduled is structurally different from a dead channel. DA's channel-continuity argument is analytically sound. Judge therefore moderates Analyst's p from 0.38 to 0.42 rather than accepting the full reduction, and maintains N4 as ACTIVE (not RESOLVED-YES).

**Falsifier for next 7 days:** Reuters/AP confirmation of a named US-Iranian bilateral meeting (any format, any location) with both governments acknowledging. If this appears, N4 resolves YES immediately per resolution criteria.
