## F1 Resolution Decision

**Ruling: RESOLVED-AMBIGUOUS → treated as NO. final_p: 0.12**

Källa A argued RESOLVE-YES on the basis of (1) PressTV June 12 ("MoU nearing completion") as implicit rescission of the June 1 suspension, and (2) Pakistan PM Shehbaz Sharif's June 14 public announcement that the US and Iran had "finalized" the MOU — both before EOD June 16 deadline.

Källa B argued AMBIGUOUS-RESOLVE-AS-NO: no formal Iranian MFA announcement rescinded the June 1 suspension before June 16; the June 11 activity was Qatar-mediated shuttle diplomacy, not a convened session between named US and Iranian principals; and the formal MOU signing was June 17 (one day after deadline).

**Judge ruling:**

The resolution criteria require either (a) a publicly confirmed HELD negotiating session between named US and Iranian principals/envoys, or (b) an official Iranian statement from MFA or listed state media (IRNA/Tasnim/Fars) that formally rescinds the June 1 suspension.

PressTV is Iranian state media but is listed by Källa A as "[STATE_MEDIA]" rather than the explicitly enumerated IRNA/Tasnim/Fars. More critically, the June 12 PressTV article reports "MoU nearing completion" — this is a progress report, not a statement formally rescinding the June 1 suspension. The distinction matters: the ambiguity rule states "one side asserting talks 'continue' without a named convened session = AMBIGUOUS-RESOLVE-AS-NO." Reporting that a deal is "nearing completion" is structurally identical to asserting talks "continue" — it describes a state, not a formal act of rescission.

The Pakistan PM's June 14 statement is an external-party characterization. It confirms talks were substantively underway, but it is not an Iranian state-media or MFA statement formally rescinding the suspension. The formal signing on June 17 would have resolved YES if it had occurred by June 16 EOD.

The event underlying the forecast (talks resumed) clearly occurred — the MOU was signed June 17. But the strict criteria and ambiguity rule favor NO. The borderline nature of this ruling is flagged for human review.

The three specialized outlets (DefenseScoop, Naval News, Army Recognition) provide corroboration. Källa A's failure to locate TIER1 coverage of the announcement reflects a research gap during a period of dense MOU-related diplomatic news, not an absence of the underlying event.

The mine-clearance mission's subsequent disruption by the July 7 conflict resumption does not affect resolution, which required only confirmed commencement within the window.

CENTCOM press release operative date: approximately June 16 (within the June 10–July 10 window). RESOLVED-YES.
