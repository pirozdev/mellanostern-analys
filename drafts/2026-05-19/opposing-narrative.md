## Judge Ruling

However, the DA overstates the weight of Araghchi's BRICS statement — Iranian FM statements at multilateral forums are routinely harder-line than negotiating-table positions, consistent with the pattern from JCPOA-2015 and JCPOA-2022 preparatory rounds. The 25% probability already prices in the significant structural barriers. Reducing to 18% would require observed evidence of channel failure, not just weakness. Probability held at 25%.

The DA's falsifier (Witkoff-Araghchi direct meeting) is added as an indicator on F5. The channel-seniority observation is logged for Sunday cold-start audit review.
