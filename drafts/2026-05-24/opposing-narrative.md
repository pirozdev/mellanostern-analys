Ghalibaf's approval means nothing if the IRGC does not comply with mine clearance or opens an alternate objection. The resolution criteria requires principal-level confirmation from Iran, not Ghalibaf — who is a legislative figure, not an executive one.

**What would prove the report wrong within 7 days:** If by May 31 no Reuters/AP/AFP article quotes a named Iranian MFA official or Iranian principal confirming the deal, or if IRNA runs a denial, the 55% probability is too high. Observable falsifier: Araghchi public statement rejecting the MOU terms as reported by Axios.

**DA recommendation:** Reduce F2 from 55% to 42%. The Ghalibaf-level approval is encouraging but insufficient for resolution criteria; the IRGC posture has not softened; and Trump's 50/50 framing on the day of the expected announcement is a material signal discount, not a noise event.
