## Narrative 1 — F5: Second reading by June 15

**Claim being opposed:** The 106-0 first reading on June 1 makes the Knesset dissolution second reading before June 15 highly likely; probability should move substantially above 0.47.

**Best contrary evidence:** Times of Israel (2026-06-02) and Haaretz report that MKs are actively debating the election date — haredi factions prefer September, Netanyahu prefers late October — and this dispute is unresolved. Under Israeli procedure, the second reading requires an election date embedded in bill text; the coalition must resolve this date dispute before second reading proceeds. JNS confirms the committee sent the bill to plenary but did not set a second-reading date. The 106-0 vote reflects all parties wanting elections but disagreeing on *when*, which may not translate into a June 15 second-reading timeline.

**What would prove the report wrong within 7 days:** Knesset Speaker announces a specific second-reading date before June 9; OR the coalition publicly agrees on an election date by June 5, removing the procedural blocker.

**DA recommendation:** Increase F5, but conservatively to ~0.62–0.65, not above 0.70 without confirmed scheduling.

### Judge ruling: PARTIAL ACCEPT
Analyst's 0.55 understates the 106-0 signal; DA's 0.62-0.65 range adopted at lower bound (0.62). The election-date dispute is confirmed unresolved and must be settled before second reading proceeds, introducing genuine scheduling uncertainty within the 9-10 working day window. Confirmed second-reading date or coalition election-date agreement would warrant upward revision to 0.65+.

Judge accepts the direction of movement (down from 0.58) but settles at 0.50 rather than Analyst's 0.48 — a midpoint that credits confirmed adverse indicators while not pre-empting June 2-3 outcome. If June 2-3 ends without communique, recommend reduction to ~0.38-0.42 at next update.

## Narrative 3 — F4: Magnitude of -22pp drop

**Claim being opposed:** Iran's negotiation suspension plus US strikes justifies a drop from 0.32 to 0.10 in a single day.

**Best contrary evidence:** Iran's prior suspension/resumption pattern (January 2025 suspended, resumed April 2025; September 2025 suspended, resumed November 2025) demonstrates that "suspension" announcements have not historically foreclosed talks within days. The IRGC's same-day public attribution statement — normally suppressed — may reflect escalation management rather than full rupture; managing the domestic narrative while keeping the diplomatic door open is consistent with prior Iranian behavior. White House officials (per PBS) had a 60-day proposal nearly signed as of May 28. Five days remain to June 7.

**What would prove the report wrong within 7 days:** Iranian MFA public statement resuming text exchange through mediators by June 4; OR Witkoff-Araghchi confirmed meeting.

**DA recommendation:** Reduce F4, but not to 0.10 — 0.18-0.20 is more calibrated given the prior suspension/resumption pattern.

### Judge ruling: PARTIAL ACCEPT
DA's historical pattern evidence is well-sourced and materially relevant. Iran's prior January 2025 and September 2025 suspension/resumption cycles are credited, limiting the floor. However, the current episode involves active kinetic exchanges on Iranian soil — not merely proxy activity — making it qualitatively more severe than prior pauses. Analyst's 0.10 is too low; DA's 0.20 ceiling is too high. Judge sets p=0.18 at DA's lower bound floor, crediting the suspension/resumption precedent while discounting that prior suspensions did not occur alongside direct bilateral strikes on Iranian territory.
