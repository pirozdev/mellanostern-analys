## Claim being opposed
Iran's June 6 drone launch toward the Strait of Hormuz represents a genuine new escalation that warrants an F1 probability increase to 55%.

## Best contrary evidence
Iran's Mehr news agency characterized the June 6 drone launches as "warning shots near the strait" most likely linked to US naval movements — not a new closure attempt. The drones were shot down before reaching any commercial vessel, meaning the de facto closure posture is unchanged from the pre-existing mine-and-warning regime in place since March 2026. Secretary Rubio's June 2 statement that Iran had mined "large segments" of the strait — itself a pre-existing condition — already priced the closure risk into inherited p=0.40. Araghchi's June 4 statement that "messages are still being exchanged" between Iran and the US via mediators is inconsistent with a party actively escalating toward formal full closure.

## What would prove the report wrong within 7 days
If no additional IRGC action occurs before EOD June 9 and F1 resolves NO, the 15pp increase from the drone incident will have been an overcorrection. A Hormuz transit count above 15/day by June 9 would further indicate the drone action did not change the closure posture.

## DA recommendation
Reduce F1 from 55% to 48% — the drone launch is a new action but does not cleanly meet the "attack on a commercial vessel" resolution criteria, and the 7-day window is short enough that base-rate continuation of existing closure posture without a new formal action should hold more weight.

## Judge ruling
REJECT. The DA's argument that drones constitute "continuation of existing posture" is not supported by the source record: CENTCOM explicitly called the drones an "immediate threat to regional maritime traffic" (not a continuation of mine-laying), and US forces struck Iranian radar sites in response — a new military exchange that exceeds the threshold of "repetition of existing warnings." The 55% reflects the increased IRGC operational tempo within the 3-day horizon window, with the ambiguity rule already adjusted to prevent a clean YES from the intercepted drones. A 48% would underweight the accelerating operational pattern documented by 3 independent TIER1 sources.
