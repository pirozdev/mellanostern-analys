## F7: Will Netanyahu's coalition lose its Knesset majority or dissolve early before 2026-10-27?

**Claim being opposed:** Netanyahu coalition stability (p=0.35 for collapse = relatively low).

**Best contrary evidence:** Dissolution bill 106-0 first reading June 2 — all coalition members voted actively for dissolution (Haaretz/ToI June 1-2). UTJ's Dov Lando ordered support; Shas instructed to vote in favor. Opposition polling at consistently 61 mandates (WAFA, Jerusalem Post). The dissolution process is now coalition-driven, not externally forced, but still satisfies F7's "early dissolution" resolution criterion.

**What would prove the report wrong:** Knesset defeats the dissolution bill at second reading, OR UTJ/Shas formally confirms continued coalition support at 61+ mandates while withdrawing support for the dissolution bill.

**DA recommendation:** 0.50.

**Judge ruling: ACCEPTED, with upward revision to 0.55.** The 106-0 dissolution bill is materially new information missed in the 20-day staleness window. The structural dependency on F6 is decisive: if F6 resolves YES (p=0.50), F7 resolves YES by definition. F6 therefore sets a p-floor of 0.50 for F7. Adding approximately 5pp for the independent forced-collapse pathway (consistent opposition polling at 61 seats) yields **F7 → 0.55**. DA's 0.50 is a valid floor; Judge revises upward to incorporate the forced-collapse tail. Note: the consensual nature of the dissolution does not exclude it from F7 resolution criteria — "early dissolution" is met regardless of whether the catalyst is coalition-driven or externally forced.
