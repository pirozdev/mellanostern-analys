## Judge Ruling

**PARTIAL ACCEPT.**

On F2: DA is technically correct that the Qatar relay does not by itself satisfy the "held session" criterion. However, the White House confirmed (Axios June 15) that Witkoff and Araghchi held a phone call on June 14 mediated by Qatari FM Al-Thani — this is a convened mediated session under the resolution rule ("direct or mediated via Oman/Qatar/Pakistan"). Judge accepts the 0.85 figure; DA's concern about the relay-vs-session distinction is addressed by this phone-call evidence.

On F8: DA's point about Supreme Leader sign-off is a real constraint. Judge accepts partial credit: p=0.72 reflects that Khamenei silence is ambiguous (could be internal deliberation toward YES, or silent veto). If Khamenei speaks negatively before June 21, F8 would fall sharply. The 0.72 figure already embeds a ~28% Khamenei-rejection/process-failure residual.

On F3 residual: DA's concern that 0.22 is too low if diplomacy collapses is noted but REJECTED for today's board. The MOU signing window (F8 horizon: June 21) is only 4 days; if it fails, F3 can be updated immediately. Locking in 0.22 is appropriate given the strength of the diplomatic evidence today.

**Falsifiers incorporated into residual probabilities:** Iran cancels Geneva meeting (reduces F8 ~−25pp), new direct exchange in 48h window (triggers F3 YES and reduces F8 ~−40pp), MCM damage/reversal (reduces F4 further, no direct impact on F2/F8). All three are reflected in the current p values' residuals.

**DA recommendation on F2 (reduce to 0.75): REJECTED.** Witkoff-Araghchi phone call mediated by Qatari FM is sufficient to meet the "convened mediated session" standard under F2's resolution criteria. The 0.85 figure holds.
