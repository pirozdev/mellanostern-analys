## Claim being opposed

"The Iran-USA Lucerne session of June 21-22 and the signed MOU framework represent a genuine breakthrough toward normalization and durable Hormuz reopening."

---

## Best contrary evidence

1. **Trump's in-session threat**: Trump threatened to "hit Iran very hard again" during the June 21 talks (CNN June 21, Fox News June 22). This is not consistent with a stable MOU framework; it reflects maximum-pressure dynamics embedded within the diplomatic process itself. The Iranian delegation temporarily walked out; VP Vance ultimately described the session as "successful" after 18 hours — but the walkout itself demonstrates framework fragility.

2. **Source conflict on what "open" means**: Iran declared Hormuz "shut" on June 25 (Al Jazeera) simultaneously with CENTCOM claiming 55 transits on the same day. This >20% source conflict is not a measurement error — it reveals a fundamental operational disagreement about who controls the transit corridor and on what terms, which is NOT resolved by the MOU text.

3. **Ghalibaf mandate concern**: The 60-day framework did not resolve enrichment and HEU stockpiles (NPR June 21). More critically, Ghalibaf (Majles Speaker) is NOT Foreign Minister Araghchi — Parliament Speakers hold no independent negotiating mandate in the Iranian constitutional system, where foreign policy authority flows from the Supreme Leader through the MFA. A Majles Speaker's presence at a negotiating table is constitutionally weaker than an MFA-signed agreement.
