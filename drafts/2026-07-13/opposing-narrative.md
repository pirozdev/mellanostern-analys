## Judge Ruling: ACCEPT

**Ruling date:** 2026-07-13

**Decision:** ACCEPT (partial — DA's structural argument accepted; DA's F3 probability increase recommendation rejected)

**Reasoning:** The DA correctly identifies that the June 17 MOU demonstrated rapid arc compression. The Swiss channel signal and Qatar/Pakistan relaunch are real and have been incorporated into F3 indicators (+10pp, +8pp, +8pp from three positive signals). However, the DA's recommendation to increase F3 above 0.50 was rejected on three grounds:

1. **Structural obstacles unreduced:** Mojtaba Khamenei's absence from public broadcast since March remains the primary unknown in all diplomatic forecasts. The June 17 MOU was concluded while Ali Khamenei was still alive and nominally in authority. The succession transition since March 9 fundamentally changed Iran's negotiating structure. No IRGC-named representative has appeared in any tracked diplomatic session.

2. **IAEA bar is new:** The Iranian parliament's July 11 national security law barring IAEA access introduces a structural obstacle absent during the June MOU negotiations. The June MOU required IAEA supervision; a second deal faces a higher implementation hurdle.

3. **F3 at 0.45 already reflects net-positive indicators:** The four indicators in F3 net approximately +14pp toward YES against -12pp (Mojtaba retribution narrative). Starting from base rate of ~35% for a ceasefire in any given 12-day window in an active conflict, 0.45 is calibrated correctly as a slightly elevated probability given the positive signals — not a sub-50 underestimate.

The DA's core narrative claim — that the MOU collapse has structural causes (IRGC autonomy, Mojtaba absence) that make a repeat deal harder than the surface-level mediator-activity suggests — is accepted and incorporated into the F3 and F5 delta_reasons.

**Observable falsifier confirmed:** Swiss-channel session before July 20 OR F2 resolves NO with >20 transits/day would trigger mandatory F3 upward revision at next update.
