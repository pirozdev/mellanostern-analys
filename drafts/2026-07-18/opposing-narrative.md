# Opposing Narrative — v48.0 (2026-07-18)

## DA's core argument (partially accepted by Judge)

The DA argued that the v47.0 scenario map (Escalation 55%, Protracted 35%, De-esc 10%) was calibrated against a cease-fire-in-progress baseline that no longer exists. The June 17 MOU, which underpinned the v47.0 framing, was declared "over" by Trump on July 8. The following structural events — not present in v47.0 — justify upward reweighting of escalation:

1. **MOU collapse (July 8)**: Trump declared the MOU void after Iran resumed enrichment activities. No replacement framework has been announced.
2. **CENTCOM three-wave strike package (July 15)**: First daylight raids included hardened military targets not struck in the June-era campaign, escalating both the targeting envelope and visible US commitment.
3. **IRGC Hormuz closure declaration (July 12)**: IRGC formally declared the Strait closed to commercial traffic, restoring the pre-MOU posture. Transits at 10/88 pre-crisis baseline vs. the 54/day peak achieved briefly in late June.
4. **Seven consecutive strike nights (July 12-18)**: No operational pause of >24h in the July 12-18 window. Tempo exceeds the pre-ceasefire June 2-10 exchange rate.
5. **IRGC 3-day ultimatum (July 18)**: Public warning of "full-scale offensive operations" if strikes persist 2-3 more days. No Washington acknowledgment.

**DA recommendation**: Escalation ≥70%, De-escalation ≤5%.

## Judge ruling on the DA argument

**PARTIAL ACCEPT.**

- **Accepted**: The structural escalation reweight is directionally correct. Escalation moved from 55% → 65%.
- **Rejected (70%+ escalation)**: The DA's ≥70% figure would implicitly override the independently calibrated F8 (22%) and F10 (20%) forecasts without new evidence beyond what was incorporated into those forecasts on July 18. The scenario-map escalation and the specific catastrophic-threshold forecasts are calibrated independently; inflating the scenario map above what the individual forecasts support would introduce internal inconsistency.
- **Rejected (de-esc ≤5%)**: F9 at 30% reflects genuine structural incentives — Iranian economic collapse from Hormuz shutdown, US domestic political costs of extended operations, Gulf-state pressure — that remain operative and were not contradicted by the opposing narrative evidence. De-escalation held at 10%.

## Final scenario probabilities

Escalation 65% / Protracted 25% / De-escalation 10%
