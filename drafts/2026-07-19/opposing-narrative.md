**What would prove the report wrong within 7 days:** If Qatar or Oman announces resumption of framework talks by July 26, OR if CENTCOM confirms no further IRGC strike activity in the Jul 19–26 window, OR if the Hormuz passage rate recovers above 14/day — any of these would validate the contrary reading and push de-escalation toward 30%+.
