With 15 days to Aug 22, all three must resolve simultaneously.

## Judge Ruling: PARTIAL ACCEPT

**Decision:** Partial accept.

**Reasoning:**  
The structural blockers identified (fee gap, IRGC non-representation, formal-publication distance) are analytically grounded and confirmed by independent sources (CNN Aug 5 analysis; TASS fee-range reporting; Gulf official CNN quote on IRGC absence). Bloomberg's "agreed in principle" headline is a genuine qualitative advance but does not satisfy F8's resolution criteria and carries the inherent Bloomberg-IRNA asymmetry flagged in Inside Iran (IRNA used softer "final stages" language, not "agreed in principle"). Källa A's 0.40 (upward from 0.38) is rejected — it over-indexed on Bloomberg's headline and understated the IRGC sign-off requirement.
