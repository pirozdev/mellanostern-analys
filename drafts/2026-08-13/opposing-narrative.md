## DA's Opposing Narrative (Källa B)

**Claim being opposed:**
A signed, mutually acknowledged US-Iran Hormuz commercial shipping framework will be publicly announced before EOD 2026-08-19 at p=0.25 (F1).

**Best contrary evidence:**
On August 9, FM Araghchi publicly declared "no direct talks with the US," confirming only indirect message-relay continues — a channel F2's own ambiguity rule classifies as non-qualifying for a formally convened session. Trump then introduced a structurally new compensation demand on August 10 (50 years of alleged Iranian actions, USS Cole families) that Iran has not accepted and that was not part of any prior framework text. Khamenei simultaneously appointed IRGC veteran Mohsen Rezaei as SNSC secretary — a hardliner with documented skepticism toward US engagement — signaling a narrowing, not widening, of Tehran's concession space. Iran's parliament national security committee passed the general framework of a rival Hormuz strategic action bill on August 10 banning US/Israeli transit; if it advances to a full chamber vote, F1's own ambiguity rule triggers a mandatory NO. All three F1 indicators remain NOT_OBSERVED or CONTRADICTED as of Aug 13; two have moved directionally away from observation in the last 48 hours, not toward it.

**What would prove the report wrong within 7 days (observable falsifier):**
Trump explicitly names and withdraws the Aug 10 compensation demand in a public statement before Aug 16; AND Oman FM issues a written readout of a signed framework text naming both a US principal and Araghchi (or Rezaei) as co-acknowledging parties; AND both US State Dept and Iran MFA/IRNA confirm the same instrument by EOD Aug 19.

**DA recommendation:**
- Reduce F1 from p=0.25 to p=0.12
- Reduce F2 from p=0.35 to p=0.25
- Hold F3 at p=0.25 (48 days remain)
- Hold F4 at p=0.25 (76-day horizon)

## Judge Ruling: ACCEPT (with override to more aggressive reductions)

**Decision:** ACCEPT

**Reasoning:** The DA correctly identified that F1 at p=0.25 was unjustifiably high. The DA's evidence — Araghchi's "no direct talks" formulation, Trump's new compensation demand, Rezaei's IRGC-veteran appointment, parliament Hormuz bill advancing, all three F1 indicators CONTRADICTED — is accurate and warranted the trigger.

The Judge rules F1→0.08 (more aggressive than DA's recommended 0.12), for the additional reason that all three F1 indicators are CONTRADICTED (not merely NOT_OBSERVED) and the remaining window is 6 days with no reversal signals. The DA's opposing narrative is accepted as correctly identifying that 0.25 was too high; the Judge's final position went further on the evidence.

For F2, the DA recommended 0.25 (reducing from 0.35). The Judge accepts the DA's underlying claim that 0.35 was unjustifiably high — ACCEPT on the claim — but rules F2 at 0.18 rather than the DA's recommended 0.25. The 13-day horizon, the explicit "exchange of messages" formulation, and Rezaei's six-condition prerequisite warrant a more aggressive cut.

For F3 and F4, the Judge overrode the DA's hold recommendation. The DA argued single-session hold for 49-day and 76-day horizons respectively. The Judge ruled that F3's deteriorating transit trend (13.5→11/day, wrong direction) and Rezaei's explicit decoupling of any Oman text from commercial reopening warrant a cut to 0.12. F4's structural deterioration (F1/F2 upstream collapse, parliament bill constraint) warrants a cut to 0.15.

This is an ACCEPT of the DA's direction on all four forecasts, not partial-accept theater. The Judge's divergence from the DA's recommended numbers is in the direction of further reduction, not disagreement with the DA's direction.
