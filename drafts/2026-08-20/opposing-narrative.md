## Claim Being Opposed
The Direction of Travel frames the situation as "active escalation, not managed pause." N7 was initially proposed at 0.30 (CENTCOM strikes inside Iran by Aug 23) and N8 at 0.45 (Iran ballistic missiles at GCC by Sep 10). DA contest: these probabilities may be too high and the "active escalation" framing may overweight a single escalation event versus the 19-night restraint pattern.

## Best Contrary Evidence
1. **19-night pause (Jul 31 – Aug 18)**: Both sides halted direct strikes for 19 consecutive nights after the MOU expired — even before Trump's formal Aug 19 non-renewal declaration. This demonstrates demonstrated capacity for mutual restraint absent a formal framework, not just MOU compliance.
2. **Rubio Aug 17 Hormuz statement**: Secretary Rubio referenced "progress on Hormuz" in a background briefing (Aug 17), suggesting a back-channel remained active even during the pause period. Active diplomatic texture contradicts a pure escalation trajectory.
3. **CENTCOM Art. 4559495 scope**: The confirmed CENTCOM strike was against "IRGC targets" — the press release does not specify Iranian territory. The most parsimonious reading is a strike on IRGC assets outside Iranian soil (Iraq, Syria, Gulf area), which is qualitatively different from strikes inside Iran and lowers N7's relevance.
4. **UAE missile attack as limited action**: Iran launched two missiles at UAE waters rather than at populated centers or military installations — this may constitute a bounded face-saving action rather than a deliberate escalation beyond ceasefire. Both parties may accept this as the new equilibrium below the threshold of full escalation.

## Falsifier
≥5 consecutive nights (through Aug 24) with no confirmed CENTCOM strikes inside Iran and no new Iranian ballistic missile launches at GCC states. If N7 and N8 both remain at NOT_OBSERVED through Aug 24, the restraint-capacity hypothesis is supported and N8's 45% should be reviewed downward.

## Judge Decision: PARTIAL ACCEPT

**Accepted**: N7 reduced from 0.30 to 0.22 — DA's trump rhetoric-vs-action gap argument and the 19-night restraint precedent justify a discount on the near-term inside-Iran-strike probability within a 72-hour window.

**Rejected**: N8 stays at 0.45. The Aug 18 UAE missile launch is a baseline data point establishing IRGC willingness and capability to launch ballistic missiles at GCC states with ceasefire terminated and no replacement framework. The 21-day window is substantially longer than the 72-hour N7 window. The historical restraint argument applies less to the 21-day horizon.

**Rejected**: Direction of Travel framing unchanged. "Active escalation, not managed pause" is the correct characterization given: ceasefire formally terminated (N5 RESOLVED-NO), CENTCOM strikes confirmed (N1 RESOLVED-YES), diplomatic track at 7% probability (N2). The DA's restraint evidence predates the Aug 18-19 events that constitute the new baseline.
