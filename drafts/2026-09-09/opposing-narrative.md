## DA Opposing Narrative (150 words)

**Judge decision:** Reduce F2 from 0.62 to 0.58 (−4pp, not the full −7pp DA recommended). The DA's core contrary evidence — slippage risk and Oman political cost — earns partial credit; the assertion that the corridor must precede the zone is rejected as inconsistent with Iranian dual-track maritime doctrine.
