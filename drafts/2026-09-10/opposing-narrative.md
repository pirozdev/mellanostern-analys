## DA Opposing Narrative B — F5 (Knesset Election Changes Plurality, horizon 2026-11-10)

**DA word limit:** 150 words

> The Analyst has anchored on the Jerusalem Post poll showing Likud at 24 vs Yashar Atid at 23 — a 1-seat Likud lead. But the Kan News poll published the same day (2026-09-09) shows the opposite: Yashar Atid at 24, Likud at 21, a 3-seat Yashar Atid lead. When two same-day polls give contradictory plurality calls, the correct response is not to average them but to widen the uncertainty band significantly. Adjusting for known pollster bias moves both toward convergence at a near-tie, which means the CURRENT plurality call is genuinely uncertain. The forecast asks whether plurality changes — but if it's already a near-tie in September, a change by November is nearly a coin flip. I'd place this at 0.35–0.38, not 0.47.

**Judge ruling:** DA's methodological critique on pollster-bias adjustment is well-taken. Analyst correctly read JP poll but missed the Kan poll divergence. Judge splits the difference at **0.40**, reflecting:
1. JP and Kan polls contradict each other — genuine near-tie in September
2. DA's pollster-bias argument narrows but does not eliminate upside for Yashar Atid
