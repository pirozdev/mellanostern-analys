## Judge Ruling

**Decision: REJECT**

The DA argument conflates "ceasefire possible" with "F4 YES possible." Reaching 30/day requires not just a ceasefire but: (1) IRGC halt in mine deployment, (2) CENTCOM mine-clearance resumption, (3) P&I insurer re-entry (days, not hours), (4) ship scheduling and routing (minimum 3-5 days from booking to transit). With 16 days to the October 3 horizon, even a ceasefire on September 17 leaves only 6-9 days of usable transit window — insufficient to generate a 7-day rolling average of 30/day. The 30/day threshold is structurally too demanding for the remaining window regardless of diplomatic movement. Judge holds F4 at 0.11.

DA's caution on the diplomatic track is noted and partially incorporated into F8 (new ceasefire by Oct 1) at p=0.18 — higher than a pure kinetic extrapolation would suggest, reflecting the rapid-pivot precedent DA cites.
