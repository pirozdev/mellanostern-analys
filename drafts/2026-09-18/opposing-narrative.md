## DA argument

The Analyst's p=0.05 floor is too close to structural impossibility. The 5-95% Codex floor exists precisely to prevent false certainty in either direction. Assigning 5% implies the 30-vessel threshold by Oct 3 is about as likely as a coin landing heads four times in a row — an extreme claim given:

1. **UNGA high-level week (Sep 18–28)** puts Pezeshkian, Araghchi, and senior IRGC-adjacent officials in New York. Prior ceasefire rounds were preceded by two weeks of back-channel contact. A rapid UNGA-brokered halt is not impossible.

2. **Iran-Oman pre-negotiated corridor plan**: Reporting from Al-Monitor (Sep 12) and Reuters (Sep 14) describes a pre-negotiated document — said to be agreed in principle between Iran's Ports Authority and Omani mediators — that would designate a protected commercial lane through the southern Strait. CENTCOM has neither confirmed nor denied. If even partially implemented, insurer confidence could recover faster than the Analyst modeled.

3. **Insurance pre-positioning**: Lloyd's of London war-risk syndicates were observed on Sep 15 quietly reviewing Hormuz pricing, per Bloomberg reporting. This is preliminary — well below a formal reinstatement — but it is evidence that the insurer community is not treating Oct 3 recovery as structurally impossible.

DA proposed range: **p=0.08–0.12**, not p=0.05.

---

## Judge ruling

**ACCEPT DA argument. p set at 0.08.**

The Analyst's p=0.05 would encode near-impossibility. The UNGA window, the Iran-Oman corridor plan, and the Lloyd's pre-positioning move constitute a small but real tail that the model must not eliminate. However, DA's upper range of 0.12 overstates that tail: each of these preconditions is itself uncertain (UNGA contact is not a session; the corridor plan is pre-negotiated but not announced; Lloyd's review is not reinstatement), and the 15-day clock is hard.

p=0.08 preserves the Codex-required non-trivial tail while reflecting that the base-case path to 30 vessels/day by Oct 3 remains deeply unfavorable.

**No change to new forecasts N1, N2, N3** — DA reviewed and accepted Analyst's calibration as within reasonable range.
