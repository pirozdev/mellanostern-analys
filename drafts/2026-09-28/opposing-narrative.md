Natural Knesset dissolution at term's end is the opposite of "early dissolution": early dissolution means Netanyahu calling elections before the mandatory term deadline. The coalition held 61 seats at dissolution, so the first branch also fails. Both YES branches were evaluated and found unsatisfied. RESOLVE-NO is correct.
