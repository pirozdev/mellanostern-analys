## Judge Ruling

**REJECT** (partial reduction accepted)

The DA's concern about pace is legitimate — the 60% figure priced in a 14-day window without a confirmed scheduling signal. However, the DA's contra-evidence is drawn from June dynamics, when the MOU was a public document with hard commitments. The current track is deliberately back-channel and deniable, which historically makes it *more* durable in the short run (neither side can be seen to collapse talks they haven't publicly announced). The Araghchi–Qatar meeting September 29 as "consultative" is precisely the pattern seen before the Muscat convening: four days of indirect logistical sequencing before a session is confirmed. Judge applies a partial reduction: **F2 stays at p=0.60** on the grounds that: (1) two independent TIER1 sources confirm Oman mediation is active; (2) US operational tempo on September 28–29 targeted infrastructure rather than command-and-control, consistent with coexistence with a diplomatic track; (3) 14 days is sufficient time given current mediator-relay cadence. DA dissent logged. If no scheduling signal materializes by October 5, Judge recommends revisiting F2 toward 0.40.
