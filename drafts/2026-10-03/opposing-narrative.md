## DA Opposing Narrative

**Claim being opposed:** Yashar's polling lead is durable and Likud's erosion represents a structural shift in Israeli electoral dynamics.

**Best contrary evidence:** Israeli pre-election polling has historically exhibited late-breaking pro-incumbent effects, particularly during active military operations. Likud underperformed polls by 3-5 seats in three of the last four elections when Israeli forces were actively engaged abroad (precedent from Apr 2019, Sep 2019, Mar 2021). The current Iran conflict has not concluded; Netanyahu's wartime incumbency premium may not yet be priced into surveys taken 18 days before the election. Additionally, four of five polls were conducted by the same two firms (Midgam, Direct Polls) whose track record on Likud shows a systematic 2-3 seat undercount against final results.

**What would prove the report wrong within 7 days:** A fifth-party poll (Panels or Smith Research) showing Likud within 2 seats of Yashar, OR Netanyahu securing a visible security achievement (hostage return, announced ceasefire) that shifts the campaign framing away from domestic corruption.

**DA recommendation:** Reduce F5 revision — maintain 0.36 rather than 0.30; the four-poll consensus overstates confidence given systematic Likud undercount in wartime conditions.

---

## Judge Ruling

**REJECT.** DA's argument rests on a historical analogy (wartime incumbency premium) that does not clearly hold here: the Iran conflict began 218 days ago and is not "new" information to voters in the way the 2019/2021 Gaza escalations were. The "systematic undercount" claim requires specific methodological evidence the DA did not cite — a directional pattern across four polls from two firms is not, by itself, evidence of systematic bias. The i24NEWS outlier (F5-friendly at 0.36) does not outweigh the four-poll composite at 0.30. **F5 stays at 0.30.**

**Weekly DA response (required ≥1x/week):** DA's core objection — that wartime elections systematically favor incumbents — is a reasonable prior. It is rejected here because the Iran conflict is now a chronic condition, not an acute emergency, and voters have had 7 months to calibrate their wartime preferences. The DA's falsifier (a fifth poll or security achievement within 7 days) is well-constructed; if it fires before Oct 21 election day, F5 should be revisited upward.
