Descriptive Delta Sep 1–5, 2026 · Day 186–190

▲ DA Opposing Narrative — Judge Accepted

The overall trajectory appears to confirm active escalation with no diplomatic off-ramp, but one underweighted residual warrants attention. Pezeshkian's September 1 SCO conditional MOU offer is structurally identical to the precondition framing that preceded the June 17 Switzerland session: a conditional "if-then" that, in June, ultimately led to a convened session. Oman FM Albusaidi's August 27 Tehran visit — the same back-channel pathway used before June — confirms that mediation bandwidth is present. An Oman-arranged session would not appear in OSINT before it occurred.

What would prove the report wrong within 7 days: any Oman or Qatar public readout (or TIER1 leak) confirming a date or location for an Iran-US indirect session before Sep 12; or Iranian state media broadcasting any official statement on a return to MOU-framework talks with named dates. Judge qualification: the June pathway required approximately 21 days of back-channel preparation before the conditional offer became a scheduled session — longer than the 7-day Sep 12 observation window. The Oman residual is real but is correctly priced at the lower end of the plausible range. This is the basis for F3 = 0.07 rather than 0.00.
